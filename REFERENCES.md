# References

This index lists all 109 works cited in *Looped Transformers: When Does Recurrence Pay Off?*, including studies of recurrent-depth models, theoretical results, baselines, benchmarks, and other background. Several configurations in the paper may come from one study.

[Browse by design choice](README.md#taxonomy) · [Download BibTeX](references.bib)

The location column points to sections of the paper. §§1–2 introduce the problem and define the scope. §§3–5 cover architecture, training, and inference. §6 compares results and deployment choices, and §7 discusses open questions. Appendices A–C expand the design axes, D covers theory, E examines empirical evidence, and F documents the review procedure. Figure 1 is indexed under §1.
